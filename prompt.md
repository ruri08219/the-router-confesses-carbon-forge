# The Router Confesses — build prompt

Competition: u-carbon-forge-the-router-confesses-f98f6b9e ("The Router Confesses", $2, FREE).
Judge: AI model head-to-head — one line, at most 200 characters, that a home
Wi-Fi router would say to excuse itself for dropping the connection at the
exact moment a movie reaches its climax. Funniest line wins; the joke must
land on its own, from a fresh angle.

Entry (103 chars, 1 line, no trailing newline):
"Sorry, I dropped the connection right at the climax — the movie was so good even I stopped to watch it."

Program: main.py (standard library only) writes exactly that text to
/output/entry.txt, validating 1–200 chars, a single line, UTF-8 encodable,
no control characters, no lone surrogates.
